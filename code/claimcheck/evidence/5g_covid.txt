COVID-19 is caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, which spreads mainly between people through respiratory droplets and aerosols. Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks. COVID-19 spread widely in many countries that had no 5G networks at all. 5G uses non-ionising radio waves, which do not carry enough energy to damage DNA or cause infection. The World Health Organization lists the story that 5G spreads COVID-19 as a myth.
