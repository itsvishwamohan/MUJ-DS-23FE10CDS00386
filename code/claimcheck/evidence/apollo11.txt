Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land on the Moon, carried out by NASA, the United States space agency. It launched on 16 July 1969 from Kennedy Space Center in Florida, and the lunar module Eagle landed in the Sea of Tranquility on 20 July 1969. Neil Armstrong became the first person to step onto the lunar surface, followed by Buzz Aldrin. Michael Collins stayed in lunar orbit aboard the command module Columbia. Armstrong and Aldrin spent about two and a half hours outside the lunar module. The astronauts collected about 21.5 kilograms of lunar rock and soil. The crew splashed down in the Pacific Ocean on 24 July 1969.
