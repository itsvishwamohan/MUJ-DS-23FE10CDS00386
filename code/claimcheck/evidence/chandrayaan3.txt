Chandrayaan-3 is an Indian lunar mission run by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). It was launched on 14 July 2023 from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota aboard an LVM3 rocket. The lander, named Vikram, touched down near the lunar south pole on 23 August 2023. This made India the fourth country to achieve a soft landing on the Moon, after the Soviet Union, the United States and China. India was also the first country to land near the lunar south pole. The mission also carried a six-wheeled rover named Pragyan. It was designed to operate for one lunar day, which is about 14 Earth days. The mission had a reported budget of roughly 615 crore rupees. Its predecessor Chandrayaan-2 lost its lander during the landing attempt in September 2019.
