Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level on Earth. It lies in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. In December 2020, Nepal and China jointly announced its official height as 8,848.86 metres. The first confirmed ascent was made on 29 May 1953 by Edmund Hillary of New Zealand and Tenzing Norgay, a Sherpa mountaineer. K2, at 8,611 metres, is the second highest mountain in the world.
