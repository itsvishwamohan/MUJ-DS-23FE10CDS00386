The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications built over many centuries by several Chinese dynasties. Most of the wall that tourists see today was built during the Ming dynasty, between 1368 and 1644. A Chinese government survey published in 2012 measured all its wall sections at about 21,196 kilometres in total. The wall is not visible to the naked eye from the Moon. Astronauts in low Earth orbit have also reported that it is very hard to see without aid, because the wall is narrow and similar in colour to the land around it. Chinese astronaut Yang Liwei said after his 2003 flight that he could not see it.
