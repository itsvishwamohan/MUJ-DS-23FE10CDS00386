The Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, on the right bank of the Yamuna river. Construction began in 1632 when the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan commissioned it in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, who died in 1631. The main mausoleum was completed around 1643, and the surrounding complex was finished around 1653. The white marble was brought from Makrana in Rajasthan. The Taj Mahal was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983. Ustad Ahmad Lahori is generally regarded as the chief architect.
