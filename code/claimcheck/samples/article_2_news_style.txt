The Taj Mahal in Agra was commissioned by Shah Jahan in 1632 for his wife Mumtaz Mahal, and its white marble was quarried at Makrana. A viral post now claims that 5G towers are spreading COVID-19 and that lightning never strikes the same place twice. Experts disagree: the Empire State Building, for example, is hit roughly 20 to 25 times each year. The same post adds that the Taj Mahal is the most visited monument in the world.
